# V1 PNG Asset Guide

## Required delivery

- Transparent PNG with no coloured, white, or checkerboard background.
- One option per file.
- Straight-on view matching the blank puppet.
- Keep every layer on the same master canvas and in the same position.
- Recommended master size: 2000 × 3000 px, portrait, exported at full size.
- Do not tightly crop garments, eyes, noses, glasses, hair, shoes, or extras.
- Keep important artwork inside the puppet silhouette and allow safe transparent space.
- Use clear black outlines and the approved simplified illustrated style.
- Avoid baked-in drop shadows outside the item unless they are part of its design.

## File naming

`category-option-name.png`

Examples:

- `outfit-blue-gingham-dress.png`
- `eyes-round-45mm-flat-plain.png`
- `glasses-square-thick-bling-xl.png`
- `shoes-pink-flower-crochet.png`

Use lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only. File names should match the app option ID.

## Layer order

1. Body
2. Outfit
3. Shoes
4. Hair
5. Eyes
6. Glasses
7. Nose
8. Extras

Some garments or accessories may need front and back pieces. Name these with
`-back` and `-front`; the app will place the body between them.

## Animation

V1 animates the assembled preview rather than each individual part. The complete
puppet can slide, bounce, zoom, turn slightly, enter, exit, and celebrate while all
PNG layers stay locked together.
